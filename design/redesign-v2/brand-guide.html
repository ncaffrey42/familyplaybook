Family Playbook

The mark is a coach's play run in the shape of a heart: a dashed route that starts at the apricot player dot, travels the whole shape, and comes back home. Warm, organized, a little sporty — a game plan for love.

Primary logo — landscape

The landscape lockup is the default. Use it in headers, marketing pages, email, and anywhere width is available. Minimum width: 160px digital / 35mm print.

Coral alternate

The coral version is for energetic, campaign-style surfaces. Keep body text and UI chrome in Mulberry so coral stays an accent, not the whole voice.

Stacked logo

Use the stacked lockup for square-ish spaces: social avatars with text, splash screens, print squares. Minimum height: 120px.

On dark backgrounds

On Mulberry or photography, use the cream reverse. The apricot dot stays apricot — it's the one spark that never changes.

Clear space & minimums

  • Clear space: keep a margin equal to the height of the apricot dot ×4 (roughly the height of the letter "F") on all sides of the logo.
  • Mark alone: use the heart-route mark by itself only once the name is established on the surface (favicons, app icon, loading states, watermarks).
  • Smallest sizes: mark 24px, landscape 160px wide, stacked 120px tall.

Typography

Fraunces — the storyteller.

Display and headings. Weights 500–600, optical size high. The wordmark in the logo files is converted to outlines, so no font install is needed to use them.

Nunito Sans — the organizer.

Body, UI, labels, checklists. Weights 400–700. Rounded and friendly at small sizes.

Both are free on Google Fonts: Fraunces · Nunito Sans

Do & don't

  • Do place the logo on Cream, Blush, white, or Mulberry.
  • Do use the mono-mulberry version for print, embossing, or single-color contexts.
  • Don't change the dash pattern, close the route into a solid heart, or remove the arrowhead — the "play" is the idea.
  • Don't recolor outside the palette, add drop shadows or outlines, or stretch the lockups.
  • Don't set the wordmark in another typeface.
